Incline Push-Up
Bodyweight Beginner 3 min
4
Sets
12
Reps
60s
Rest
Muscles Targeted
Chest Front Delts Triceps Core Serratus Anterior
Instructions
1
Place your hands on an elevated surface (bench, box, or wall) slightly wider than shoulder-width apart.
2
Step back so your body forms a straight line from head to heels. Engage your core throughout the movement.
3
Slowly lower your chest toward the surface, keeping elbows at roughly 45° from your torso.
4
Press back up to the starting position, fully extending your arms without locking your elbows.
